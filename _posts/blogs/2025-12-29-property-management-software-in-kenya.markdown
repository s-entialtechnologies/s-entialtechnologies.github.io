## Why Kenyan Businesses Need Property Management Software

Property management software helps landlords and property managers improve efficiency, reduce errors, and save time. Here’s why it’s essential:

### 1. Centralized Tenant Management
> Keep all tenant data organized and accessible.  

- Track tenant information, leases, and rental history  
- Manage multiple properties from one platform  
- Send reminders for lease renewals and rent collection  

### 2. Automate Rent Collection
> Ensure timely and accurate payments.  

- Generate invoices and track payments automatically  
- Send alerts for overdue rent  
- Integrate with accounting or payroll systems for smooth operations  

### 3. Simplify Maintenance Management
> Keep properties in top condition.  

- Schedule and track maintenance tasks  
- Maintain service history for each property

- Analyze trends to maximize rental income
