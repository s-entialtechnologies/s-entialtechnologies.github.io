Garage Storytelling System Pricing

1. Executive Summary

Thank you for considering S-Ential Technologies as your partner in digital growth. Nairobi’s automotive repair industry is highly competitive, and customers have many choices but very little trust.

The Garage Storytelling System is built specifically for auto repair shops to:

  • Build trust with real repair stories
  • Showcase expertise without technical jargon
  • Attract first-time customers
  • Increase repeat visits
  • Keep your social pages consistently active
  • Turn everyday work in the shop into powerful content

Our approach combines story-driven content, reputation building, and consistent visibility to help your garage stand out.

2. Objectives

  • Increase customer trust: Through real case stories, transparent repairs, technician introductions, and educational breakdowns.
  • Bring in more high-value jobs: Focus on diagnostics, suspension, engine work, brakes, and major repairs.
  • Keep your online presence active: Consistent storytelling content across social media.
  • Improve Google visibility: More photos, reviews, posts, and accurate information.
  • Generate more bookings: Story-based call-to-actions encouraging inspections and appointments.

3. Our System: How It Works

PART 1 - STORY DISCOVERY

We uncover your most powerful shop stories:

  • Why you started
  • Customer transformation stories
  • Technician experience
  • Your philosophy on honesty and transparency
  • Community involvement

What Story Discovery Really Means

Most garages already have amazing stories happening every day, but they never get shared. Story Discovery helps us pull those stories out by interviewing you, learning how the garage started, understanding your values, and identifying the types of repair cases that build trust with customers.

We use these insights to create a unique content voice for your garage so your social media doesn’t look generic or templated. This becomes your long-term storytelling foundation across all platforms.


PART 2 - STORY CAPTURE

We give you a simple way to send us:

  • Short clips
  • Repair photos
  • Voice notes of interesting cases

We handle all editing and copywriting.


PART 3 - STORY CREATION

We create professional, trust-building content:

  • Repair case stories
  • Educational breakdowns
  • Technician features
  • Behind-the-scenes reels
  • Carousels and photos

PART 4 - STORY AMPLIFIER

We turn your stories into actual business:

  • Consistent posting
  • Strong call-to-actions
  • Review generation
  • Monthly analytics
